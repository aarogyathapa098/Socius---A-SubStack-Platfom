# Socius Design Direction

## Inspiration Source
The `stitch` folder points toward:
- oversized serif headlines
- asymmetrical content layouts
- soft rounded panels
- glassy navigation surfaces
- editorial whitespace
- restrained burgundy and green accents

## What Socius Keeps
- premium editorial mood
- layered sections instead of heavy borders
- high-contrast serif display typography
- floating cards with soft radii
- community-first landing and reading experiences

## What Socius Changes
- different font pairing
- different page rhythm and panel composition
- different palette values
- different labels, community names, and copy
- different component hierarchy and card structure

## Socius Visual System
- Display font: `Cormorant Garamond`
- Body font: `Manrope`
- Background: warm parchment rather than cool white
- Primary accent: mulberry red
- Secondary accent: deep pine
- Surface logic: layered ivory cards, blurred glass navigation, quiet gradients

## Ethical Community Theme
The visual tone should feel thoughtful and civic, not trendy or gamer-like. The product should read as:
- calm
- intelligent
- intentional
- trustworthy
- discussion-led
